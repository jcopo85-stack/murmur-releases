# Murmur

Private push-to-talk dictation for macOS. Hold **fn**, speak, and let go: Murmur turns your speech into text on your Mac and pastes it wherever you're typing. Nothing leaves your computer.

## Download

**[Download Murmur for Mac](https://github.com/jcopo85-stack/murmur-releases/raw/main/Murmur.dmg)**

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later. AI cleanup, per-app styles, command mode and transforms need macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on.

## Install

1. Open the DMG and drag Murmur to Applications.
2. Open Murmur. The first time, macOS may say it can't check Murmur for malicious software, because this build isn't notarized by Apple. Click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.
3. Murmur walks you through a one-minute setup.

Murmur keeps itself up to date: it checks for new versions once a day and asks before installing.
